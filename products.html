Delivery & Returns

  • Standard Delivery: 3–5 business days – FREE on orders over R800, otherwise R80.
  • Express Delivery: 1–2 business days – R150.
  • Same-day Collection: Available from our Bree Street store (Joburg).
  • Orders placed before 12pm are dispatched the same day (weekdays).
  • Tracking details sent via email and SMS upon dispatch.
  • 30-day return policy on all unworn, unwashed items.
  • Items must have original tags attached and be in original packaging.
  • Exchange for a different size is free of charge.
  • Refunds processed within 5–7 business days to original payment method.
  • Sale and customised items are non-refundable.
