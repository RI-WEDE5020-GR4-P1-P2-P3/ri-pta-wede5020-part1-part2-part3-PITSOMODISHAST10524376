Why Urban Threads?

To provide premium streetwear that reflects urban culture while empowering individuals to express their unique style. Urban Threads was born in Johannesburg with a simple mission: bring authentic, high-quality streetwear to South Africa.

Frequently Asked Questions

We stock sizes XS through to 3XL across all our clothing ranges. Size guides are available on each product page. If you are unsure, please use our enquiry form and we will help you find the right fit.

Standard delivery across South Africa takes 3–5 business days. Express delivery (1–2 days) is available at checkout for an additional fee. Orders placed before 12pm are dispatched the same day.

We offer a 30-day return policy on all unworn, unwashed items with the original tags attached. Items must be returned in their original packaging. Customised or sale items are non-refundable.
