Who We Are

Urban Threads was founded in 2018 in Johannesburg with a simple yet powerful idea: South Africa deserved its own authentic streetwear brand — one that understood local culture, spoke the local language, and celebrated the creativity of South African youth.

What started as a small screen-printing operation in a Braamfontein garage quickly grew into a fully fledged fashion brand. By 2020, Urban Threads was stocked in several boutiques across Johannesburg and Cape Town. Today, we ship nationwide and operate our flagship store on Bree Street in Joburg's creative district.

Fashion is more than just clothing — it is a form of self-expression, and Urban Threads exists to give every South African a voice through what they wear.

Our mission is to provide premium streetwear that reflects urban culture while empowering individuals to express their unique style. We are committed to creating high-quality garments that are both fashionable and meaningful — pieces that tell a story.

Every product we release is designed with the urban South African in mind: bold, confident, and unapologetically authentic.

To become South Africa's leading streetwear brand, recognised nationally and internationally for quality, authenticity, and cultural impact. We aim to inspire a new generation of creators, entrepreneurs, and trendsetters across the African continent.

Our small but passionate team includes designers, photographers, logistics specialists, and street culture enthusiasts — all united by a love for fashion and community.

We work with local artists and creatives to design every collection, ensuring that Urban Threads remains a true reflection of South African street culture.

Our Values

Every Urban Threads garment is made with premium materials and subject to strict quality control. We believe that great streetwear should last — not just look good for one season. We use ring-spun cotton, reinforced stitching, and high-quality inks for all prints.

We do not follow trends — we set them. Every collection draws inspiration directly from South African townships, urban landscapes, and the vibrant local art scene. Our designs are a love letter to the streets that raised us.

Urban Threads is more than a brand — it is a community. We host pop-up events, support local artists, and partner with community organisations in Johannesburg. A portion of every sale goes towards youth creative development programmes.

We are committed to reducing our environmental footprint. By 2027, we aim to use 100% recycled or organic cotton in all our products. We work only with ethical suppliers who pay fair wages and maintain safe working conditions.
